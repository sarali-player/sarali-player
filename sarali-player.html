        Sarali Privacy Policy
        Local by default
        By default, your music, practice files, practice sections, notes, loops, tempo settings, and other practice details remain on your device. Sarali does not use cookies. Sarali stores practice preferences and optional local records using browser storage on your device. This information is not sent to the Sarali maintainer.
        Embedded YouTube and Google Forms may use their own cookies or service data under their respective policies and browser settings.
        Optional usage insights
        Help improve Sarali is off by default. If enabled, Sarali records approved control names, their order and counts, how controls are presented, a broad device class, app version, and active interaction time that excludes time while Sarali is hidden, plus completion, cancellation, repeated-action, or error signals. Raw event timelines and exact timing are never included in the feedback summary. These records are used only to improve navigation, control placement, accessibility, workflows, and the player experience—not for advertising, profiling, or sale.
        Usage records never contain music, recordings, filenames, song titles or links, practice files, section starts, section names, notes, text you enter, exact click coordinates, or persistent device identifiers. They remain on this device for up to 30 days, 1,000 events, or approximately 200 KB.
        Sharing with feedback
        Usage records are never sent automatically. When you send feedback, Sarali offers a separate choice to preview and attach a compact summary. Google Forms requests your email, name, rating, and comments, so an attached summary may be associated with those responses. Sarali never submits the form automatically.
        Your choices and retention
        You can disable recording or delete local records in Settings. Local deletion does not remove feedback already submitted. Submitted usage summaries are retained for no more than 12 months. To request deletion of a submitted response, email sarali.player@gmail.com.
