      Sarali Privacy Policy
      
                
      Local by default
      
                
      By default, your music, practice files, timepoints, segments, notes, loops, tempo settings, and other practice details remain on your device and are not sent to the Sarali maintainer.
      
                
      Optional usage insights
      
                
      Help improve Sarali is off by default. If enabled, Sarali records approved control names, their order and counts, how controls are presented, a broad device class, app version, approximate elapsed-time ranges, and completion, cancellation, repeated-action, or error signals. These records are used only to improve navigation, control placement, accessibility, workflows, and the player experience—not for advertising, profiling, or sale.
      
                
      Usage records never contain music, recordings, filenames, song titles or links, practice files, timepoints, segment names, notes, text you enter, exact click coordinates, or persistent device identifiers. They remain on this device for up to 30 days, 1,000 events, or approximately 200 KB.
      
                
      Sharing with feedback
      
                
      Usage records are never sent automatically. When you send feedback, Sarali offers a separate choice to preview and attach a compact summary. Google Forms requests your email, name, rating, and comments, so an attached summary may be associated with those responses. Sarali never submits the form automatically.
      
                
      Your choices and retention
      
                
      You can disable recording or delete local records in Settings. Local deletion does not remove feedback already submitted. Submitted usage summaries are retained for no more than 12 months. To request deletion of a submitted response, email sarali.player@gmail.com.
